## About

I am building a career in finance by combining **investment decision-making, financial modelling, and data analysis**. My RMIT coursework has involved corporate valuation, equity research, bond portfolio analysis, and econometric models. Here I turn selected academic projects into clearly documented case studies so that readers can examine the assumptions, methods, findings and limitations.

## Featured finance work

### [Corporate Financial Management — RMIT BAFI3257](https://github.com/Pratham8901/corporate-financial-management-project-valuation)

| Case study | Business question | Techniques |
|---|---|---|
| [01 · Project Valuation & Real Options](https://github.com/Pratham8901/corporate-financial-management-project-valuation/tree/main/assessment-1-project-valuation) | When does an AI subscription-platform investment create value? | DCF · NPV · 10-year forecasts · sensitivity analysis · expansion/abandonment options |
